Tutedude · SEO assignment

SEO Mastery

My Tutedude SEO assignment covers SEO fundamentals, keyword research, on-page and off-page SEO. For the practical part, I audited my practice site GrowLocal Digital and wrote an SEO blog post and a link-building plan.

SEO FundamentalsKeyword ResearchOn-Page SEOOff-Page SEOTechnical SEO

Website audit: GrowLocal Digital

  • Page title is 118 characters, too long. Google cuts titles at about 60, so I suggested a 57-character version.
  • The H1 has no target keyword. I suggested a keyword-rich H1 for Delhi NCR businesses.
  • Heading order and language tag need fixing (lang en-US vs en-IN).
  • PageSpeed (23 Oct 2026): Performance 96 mobile and 97 desktop, SEO 100.

Keyword research

  • Researched 9 keywords for digital marketing, local SEO and Google Ads services in Delhi NCR.
  • Split them into short-tail and long-tail, with volume, difficulty and CPC.
  • Mapped each keyword to a page on the site, starting with the easier ones.

SEO blog post

  • Topic: digital marketing career in India, focus keyword used in title, H1, H2s and body.
  • Meta title (59 characters) and meta description (150 characters) written to match the search intent.
  • Includes an internal link, an external link and an image with alt text. About 526 words.

Link-building plan

  • White hat methods only: guest posting, resource-page and broken-link building, and digital PR.
  • Monthly targets for outreach, guest posts and placements, with a sample outreach email.
  • Tracking progress monthly in Google Search Console.
